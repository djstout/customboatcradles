What kinds of boats can you build cradles for?+

We make steel frames for pontoons, tritoons, and variations of V-hull boats. Each cradle is designed to the boat manufacturer’s specifications.

Can a cradle adjust for a different boat later?+

Our cradles are designed to be adjustable for a new boat of similar design. Tell us what you have now and what you may move to.

Do you install and service lift systems?+

Yes. We install, repair, and maintain complete lift systems, including cable changes, lubrication, motor or switch repair, and cradle installation.

Where do you work?+

We’re a family-owned local business serving the Cedar Creek Lake area. Call to talk through your location and project.
